# キャラクター生成プロンプト (ヤコブとエサウ③)

## 共通の画風（全プロンプトに追加）
`Watercolor style illustration, Soft pastel colors, Hand-drawn texture, soft edges, Warm and gentle lighting, glowing light particles, soft sparkles, Dreamlike atmosphere, warm and cozy, Simple characters with innocent facial expressions, Large and expressive eyes`

---

## 1. ヤコブ (Jacob)
**Prompt:**
`Jacob, an adult man with a gentle and determined expression, large and expressive eyes, wearing simple earth-tone natural clothing, soft rounded features, light beard, holding a walking stick, watercolor style, soft pastel colors, glowing light particles, cozy atmosphere`

## 2. エサウ (Esau)
**Prompt:**
`Esau, an adult man with a strong but gentle and forgiving smile, large and expressive eyes, wearing simple reddish-brown natural clothing, light beard, muscular but soft features, watercolor style, soft pastel colors, glowing light particles, cozy atmosphere`

## 3. ふしぎな人 (Mysterious Person / Angel)
**Prompt:**
`A mysterious majestic person wrapped in soft glowing light, gentle expression, large expressive eyes, wearing simple white flowing robes, glowing light particles, soft sparkles, watercolor style, soft pastel colors, dreamlike atmosphere`
