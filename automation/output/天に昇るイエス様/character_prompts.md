# Character Prompts for Flow

## Jesus
Jesus, a kind and gentle man with a warm smile, long brown hair, soft beard, wearing simple flowing white robes. Soft rounded features, large expressive eyes. Surrounded by a gentle, glowing light. Watercolor style illustration, soft pastel colors, dreamlike atmosphere, warm and cozy.

## Disciples
A group of gentle disciples with innocent facial expressions, large and expressive eyes, wearing simple earth-tone clothing (brown, light blue, green). Soft rounded features. Watercolor style illustration, soft pastel colors, dreamlike atmosphere, warm and cozy.

## Angel
A gentle angel wearing glowing white robes, with soft white wings and a reassuring smile. Large expressive eyes, innocent facial expressions. Surrounded by soft sparkles and light. Watercolor style illustration, soft pastel colors, dreamlike atmosphere.
