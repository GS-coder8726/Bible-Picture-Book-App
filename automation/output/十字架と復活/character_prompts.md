# キャラクタープロンプト集（crucifixion）

## Jesus (イエスさま)
[Jesus]
A loving and gentle man named Jesus, wearing simple earth-tone robes (white or light brown), warm eyes, soft facial features, shoulder-length wavy hair, a small beard. Watercolor style illustration, soft pastel colors, hand-drawn texture, warm and gentle lighting. Simple character with innocent facial expression.

## Disciples (おともだち/弟子たち)
[Disciples]
Simple characters of friends, innocent facial expressions, large and expressive eyes, wearing simple natural clothing in earth tones. Watercolor style illustration, soft pastel colors, hand-drawn texture, warm and cozy.

## Angel (てんし)

A beautiful glowing angel, wearing glowing white robes, warm gentle smile, soft glowing wings, glowing light particles. Watercolor style illustration, soft pastel colors, hand-drawn texture, dreamlike atmosphere.
