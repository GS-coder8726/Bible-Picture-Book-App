# Character Prompts for Flow

## Style Guidelines (Always apply these to the base prompt)
- Watercolor style illustration, soft pastel colors, hand-drawn texture, soft edges, warm and gentle lighting, glowing light particles, soft sparkles, dreamlike atmosphere, warm and cozy

## Moses
**Prompt:**
A cute older man named Moses with a long white beard, holding a wooden staff, wearing simple earth-tone brown robes, soft rounded features, large expressive eyes, innocent expression. Watercolor style illustration, soft pastel colors, hand-drawn texture, soft edges, warm and gentle lighting, glowing light particles, soft sparkles, dreamlike atmosphere, warm and cozy.

## Israelites (with children)
**Prompt:**
A group of people, men, women, and children with chibi-like proportions, wearing simple natural earth-tone clothing, holding small bundles. Soft rounded features, large expressive eyes, innocent expressions. Watercolor style illustration, soft pastel colors, hand-drawn texture, soft edges, warm and gentle lighting, glowing light particles, soft sparkles, dreamlike atmosphere, warm and cozy.
