# Character Prompts

### Art Style (共通)
Watercolor style illustration, soft pastel colors, hand-drawn texture, soft edges, warm and gentle lighting, glowing light particles, soft sparkles, dreamlike atmosphere, warm and cozy

### Jesus (イエスさま)
Warm and gentle smiling man, brown hair, simple light-colored earth-tone robe, peaceful expression, soft rounded features

### The Boy (男の子)
Innocent small boy, large expressive eyes, wearing simple natural earth-tone clothing, holding a small woven basket, cute chibi-like proportions

### Disciples (お弟子たち)
Simple men, soft rounded features, wearing natural earth-tone tunics, looking amazed or helpful

### The Crowd (群衆)
A large gathering of people, families and children, sitting on soft green grass, simple characters, peaceful atmosphere
